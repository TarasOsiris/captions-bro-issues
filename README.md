# Captions Bro: Auto Subtitles

**Video editor with automatic captions and a built-in teleprompter, for iPhone.**

[Website](https://captionsbro.app) · [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6770987720) · [Watch the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7kjWsrV3Fjs) · [Support](https://captionsbro.app/support) · [Privacy Policy](https://captionsbro.app/privacy/)

Captions Bro turns raw phone footage into finished vertical video. It gives you automatic subtitles, karaoke-style word highlighting, a real editing timeline and a teleprompter, all in one app.

Record with the teleprompter or pull clips in from Photos. The app transcribes the speech on device, and then you style the captions. What you see in the preview is exactly what gets exported.

[![Captions Bro video](https://img.youtube.com/vi/7kjWsrV3Fjs/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7kjWsrV3Fjs)

## Features

### Automatic subtitles and captions
- Subtitles generated from speech automatically, with transcription running on device
- Karaoke-style highlighting that lights up each word as it is spoken
- Bold caption templates ready for social, with fonts, colors, outlines and shadows
- Add punctuation, remove filler words and regenerate any line
- Split, merge, retime, restyle and correct captions by hand
- Support for mixed-language speech through a secondary language

### A real editing timeline
- Multi-track timeline: trim, split, reorder, duplicate and delete clips
- Speed, reverse, freeze frames and transitions between clips
- Keyframe animation for position, scale, rotation and opacity
- Text, image and video overlays, with masks and background removal
- Color grades, filters and effects
- Noise reduction, voiceovers and sound effects
- 9:16, 4:5 and square reframing for every platform

### Teleprompter
Write or import a script, set the scroll speed and record yourself reading it while the camera rolls. It is built for formats where the words matter more than the b-roll: talking-head videos, explainers, tutorials, UGC, course clips and sales videos.

### Export and share
- MP4 or MOV export with the captions burned in
- Resolution and frame rate to match where you are posting
- Save to Photos, or share to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Messages

## Why creators use it

Most people watch with the sound off, and captions are what keep them watching. Typing captions out by hand is the slowest part of publishing anything. Captions Bro handles the transcription, the timing and the styling, and leaves the editing to you.

Everything runs on your iPhone, so a weak signal never stops you working.
