# Captions Bro — Auto Subtitles, Video Editor & Teleprompter for iPhone

**[captionsbro.app](https://captionsbro.app)** — Turn raw phone footage into finished vertical video. Automatic captions with karaoke-style word highlighting, a real multi-track editing timeline and a built-in teleprompter, all in one iPhone and iPad app. Editing and rendering happen entirely on your device.

Captions Bro is built for creators who publish short-form video on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook. Record yourself with the teleprompter or pull clips in from Photos. The app transcribes the speech, times every word, and gives you styled, animated captions you can fine-tune on a timeline. What you see in the preview is exactly what gets exported.

## Automatic Captions

Tap **Captions** and Captions Bro listens to your video and writes the subtitles for you, timed to each word.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Speech-to-text** | Uses Apple's speech recognition, and the newer **SpeechAnalyzer** engine on iOS 26+. Recognition runs on-device whenever your language supports it. |
| **Language choice** | Pick any language your device supports, or let the app detect it automatically. |
| **Secondary language** | For videos that mix two languages, add a second language and both passes are merged into one set of captions. |
| **Add punctuation** | Adds commas, periods and question marks automatically. |
| **Remove filler words** | Drops "um", "uh", "er", "hmm" and similar. On by default, and can be run again on existing captions. |
| **Mask profanity** | Hides swear words in the generated captions. |
| **Split at sentence end** | Starts a new caption at every sentence boundary. |
| **Smart project names** | Names each project from what was said. Uses Apple Intelligence on iOS 26+. |

### Editing captions

- Edit any caption's text inline
- **Split** a caption, or split it by line
- **Join** a caption with the previous one
- **Insert** a new caption anywhere
- **Retime** by trimming or dragging the caption block on the timeline
- **Regenerate** captions after changing settings
- Delete single captions, a multi-selection or all of them

### Caption layout

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| Capitalization | Original case, UPPERCASE, lowercase, Capitalized |
| Words per caption | 2–8 (default 4) |
| Max lines | 1–3 (default 2) |
| Caption hold | 0–3 s: how long a caption stays on screen after its last word |
| Position & size | Default placement and size (0.5×–2×) for new captions |
| Punctuation | Show or hide |

## Caption Styles & Animations

### Templates

Start from one of **19 ready-made caption templates**, each a complete look with font, color, effects and animation:

Classic · Bold · **Hype** · Highlight · Typewriter · Chroma · Karaoke Pop · Cursor · Pop · Marker Pop · Glow Sweep · Box Reveal · Fade Reveal · Sticker · Bubble · Spotlight · Headline · Soft Pop · Neon

The **Bold** template can also add a matching emoji that bounces in above each caption. Apply a style to one caption or to all of them, and copy one caption's placement to the rest.

### Style editor

| Tab | What you can change |
|-----|---------------------|
| **Font** | Any of the 51 bundled fonts or your own imported fonts |
| **Color** | Preset swatches or a custom color picker |
| **Style** | About 100 text style presets in 7 groups: Basic, Glow, Red, Blue, Yellow, Pink, Green |
| **Align** | Text alignment |
| **Animate** | Text animations and karaoke effects (see below) |
| **Outline** | Stroke color and width |

Styles support outlines (including multiple outlines), glow, a background box, drop shadows, vertical or horizontal gradient fills, hollow letters and textures (speckles, scratches, scanlines). Opacity, scale (0.3×–5×), rotation and mirroring can be set per caption.

### Karaoke highlighting

The word being spoken gets its own color, and can also get a rounded box behind it and scale up. Pick one of **8 word-by-word effects**:

Highlight · Karaoke Pop · Multicolor Jump · Chroma · Beats · Flash Shaking · Ripple · Glow

### Text animations

**50+ whole-caption animations**, including Typewriter, Cursor, Glitch In/Out, Wavy Bounce, Bounce In, Spring In/Out, Zoom In/Out, Roller Coaster, Curtains Open/Close, Flip In/Out, Light Flow, Shake, Random Reveal, Phantom In/Out, Dissolve In/Out, Wobbly Projector, Wave, Blow Up, Color Glitch and Flutter In/Out.

## Editing Timeline

A multi-track timeline modeled on the editors creators already know from TikTok and CapCut.

### Clip tools

| Tool | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Trim** | Drag either edge of a clip to shorten or extend it |
| **Split** | Cut a clip in two at the playhead |
| **Duplicate** | Copy a clip in place |
| **Replace** | Swap in new footage and keep the clip's edits |
| **Delete** | Remove a clip |
| **Reorder** | Drag clips to rearrange them |
| **Speed** | 0.1× to 10×. Captions stay in sync with the footage. |
| **Reverse** | Play a clip backwards |
| **Freeze** | Insert a freeze frame |
| **Crop** | Freeform, 9:16, 16:9, 1:1, 3:4, 4:3 |
| **Rotate / Mirror** | Rotate or flip footage |
| **Mask** | Line, Mirror, Circle, Rectangle, **Text** (video shows through the letters) or **Custom SVG**. Masks can be inverted. |
| **Opacity** | Fade a clip against what's behind it |
| **Volume** | 0–100% or mute |
| **Extract audio** | Pull the audio out onto its own track |
| **Reduce noise** | Gentle filter that cuts low rumble and high hiss |
| **Animation** | In, Out and Combo clip animations (see below) |
| **Cutout** | Remove the background (see [Overlays](#overlays-stickers--background-removal)) |
| **Overlay** | Turn a main-track clip into an overlay, and back |

### Clip animations

- **44 In** and **44 Out** animations, such as Slice In, Folding Fan, Black Hole, Retro TV, Puzzle, Fade, Zoom In and Bounce
- **31 Combo** animations, such as Dynamic Shake, Pendulum, Origami, Throbbing Zoom and Spin Fall

### Workflow

- Unlimited **undo / redo**
- **Snapping** that you can turn on or off
- Hide any timeline row with the eye toggle
- **Safe-area guide** so captions don't end up under platform UI
- A **customizable toolbar**: reorder its actions and change the button size
- **Layers** sheet for stacking order

## Privacy

- **Your footage stays on your device.** Importing, editing and rendering all happen locally, and nothing is uploaded to Captions Bro.
- **Transcription** uses Apple's speech recognition and runs on-device for every language your device supports. For other languages, Apple's servers may process the audio.
- Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports contain counts and settings, **never your video or the words you type**.
- Permissions requested: camera, microphone, speech recognition and saving to Photos.

Full policy: [captionsbro.app/privacy](https://captionsbro.app/privacy/)

## FAQ

**How do I get captions?**
Open a project and tap **Captions**. Captions Bro transcribes the speech and places timed captions on the timeline. Pick a template, then edit any line by hand.

**Which languages can it transcribe?**
Any language supported by Apple's speech recognition on your device. For bilingual videos, set a secondary language.

**Does my video leave my phone?**
No. Editing and rendering happen on your device. Transcription runs on-device where your language supports it.

**Why did my captions move when I changed speed?**
Captions follow the footage. When a clip speeds up or slows down, its captions are retimed so they stay in sync with the words.

**What should I export for TikTok or Reels?**
9:16 canvas, MP4, 1080p and 30 fps is the safe choice. Use 4K / 60 fps if your source footage supports it.

**Where does my export go?**
Tap **Download** to save it to Photos, or **More** to share it straight to any app.

**Can I export in HDR?**
Not at the moment. Exports are SDR H.264, which every platform plays correctly.

**Export won't start. What do I do?**
Free up some storage on your device. Exports need temporary space while rendering.

**What does PRO change?**
PRO removes the one-project limit. Every editing tool, effect, font and export option is available on the free tier.
